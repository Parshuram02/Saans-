Empirical Validation

Leave-One-Year-Out Evaluation

To evaluate prediction accuracy, Breathe Ahead was back-tested across 22 test days during peak burning seasons (Oct 20 – Nov 20) in 2024 and 2025. Each test year was excluded from the historical climatology index during evaluation.

Method Precision@50 Recall@50 Mechanism
Breathe Ahead Combined Model 37.7% 37.0% Historical prior + 48h spatial diffusion
Yesterday's Fires (Persistence) 24.6% 23.9% 24h raw fire persistence
History Only (Climatology) 41.3% 40.8% Multi-year ±7d seasonal window
Key Finding: Simple persistence ("Yesterday's fires") yields 24.6% precision because burning shifts spatially as harvest progresses across districts. Combining multi-year seasonal priors with recent neighborhood ignition diffusion yields superior spatial coverage.

System Architecture & Methodology

Explicit mathematical formulation with transparent parameters

01

NASA FIRMS VIIRS Ingestion

Thermal anomaly observations from SNPP and NOAA-20 VIIRS sensors (375m nadir resolution). Processes nominal and high-confidence detections, extracting spatial coordinates, timestamps, and Fire Radiative Power (FRP in MW).

02

Spatial Risk Formulation

Partitioned into a 0.1° (~10 km) resolution grid covering Punjab and Haryana. Combines multi-year historical fire density (±7 day seasonal window) with 48-hour neighborhood diffusion weighted by log-scale FRP.

03

Smoke Transport & Arrival ETA

Identifies upwind fire clusters within 600 km using surface wind vectors. Calculates transport time ($D / V_{\text{wind}}$) using FRP-weighted median aggregation to compute city-level threat scores and arrival ETAs.

Known Scope & Limitations

  • Satellite Overpass Intervals: VIIRS sensors observe northern India twice daily. Ignitions between passes or under thick cloud cover have delayed detection.
  • Heuristic Estimation: The risk score combines empirical spatial and seasonal priors; it is not a full 3D atmospheric dispersion model (e.g. HYSPLIT).
  • First-Order Wind Transport: Arrival ETA assumes linear trajectory from surface wind vectors. Boundary layer dynamics and multi-level shear are simplified.
  • Threat Thresholds: Operational threat levels (50 and 200) serve triage purposes and are tuned against historical plume events.
